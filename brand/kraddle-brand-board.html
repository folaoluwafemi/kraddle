Mark

Square ends. Solid and reliable.

The mark is your designer's original: two identical halves, each turned half a turn onto the other, with square ends. The name is set in lowercase Azeret Mono at weight 800, one step lighter than the mark so the two read as the same weight.

LockupMark height is 0.82 × the name's font size. The gap between them is 0.32 × the font size.
  • Ink on paper, or paper on ink. The logo itself is never orange or green.
  • Keep clear space of at least half the mark's width on every side.
  • Smallest size: the mark 16px tall on screens. Below that, use the mark without the name.
  • Never round the ends, stretch, outline, or add shadows or gradients.
  • The name is always lowercase: kraddle.
  • The mark alone works as the app icon, favicon and avatar.
